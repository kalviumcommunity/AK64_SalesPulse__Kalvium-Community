# KPI Reference Document

This document defines the core business KPIs used by the SalesPulse project. Each KPI includes a formal definition, formula, data source, target range, owner, update frequency, and notes so every team can calculate the same metric in the same way.

## 1. Monthly Active Users (MAU)
- **Definition**: Distinct customers with at least one transaction in the last 30 days.
- **Formula**: `COUNT(DISTINCT customer_id) WHERE transaction_date >= TODAY() - 30 days`
- **Data Source**: Transactions table (`customer_id`, `transaction_date`)
- **Target Range**: 5,000 - 6,000
- **Owner**: Product Manager
- **Update Frequency**: Daily
- **Notes**: Indicator of product engagement; seasonal dips in Q4.

## 2. Revenue per Customer
- **Definition**: Average revenue generated by each unique customer.
- **Formula**: `SUM(amount) / COUNT(DISTINCT customer_id)`
- **Data Source**: Transactions table (`customer_id`, `amount`)
- **Target Range**: $90 - $110
- **Owner**: Finance Manager
- **Update Frequency**: Daily
- **Notes**: Useful for monitoring monetization quality and pricing effectiveness.

## 3. Churn Rate
- **Definition**: Share of customers active in the prior period who do not return in the current period.
- **Formula**: `Customers active in period 1 and inactive in period 2 / Customers active in period 1`
- **Data Source**: Transactions table (`customer_id`, `transaction_date`)
- **Target Range**: 0% - 5%
- **Owner**: Customer Success Lead
- **Update Frequency**: Weekly
- **Notes**: High churn indicates retention friction or product dissatisfaction.

## 4. Average Order Value (AOV)
- **Definition**: Average value of a single completed order.
- **Formula**: `SUM(amount) / COUNT(transaction_id)`
- **Data Source**: Transactions table (`transaction_id`, `amount`)
- **Target Range**: $75 - $125
- **Owner**: Sales Ops Lead
- **Update Frequency**: Daily
- **Notes**: Helpful for pricing, upsell, and basket-size analysis.

## 5. Repeat Purchase Rate
- **Definition**: Share of customers who made more than one purchase in the observed period.
- **Formula**: `COUNT(customer_id with >1 purchase) / COUNT(DISTINCT customer_id)`
- **Data Source**: Transactions table (`customer_id`)
- **Target Range**: 25% - 45%
- **Owner**: Growth Lead
- **Update Frequency**: Weekly
- **Notes**: Measures habit formation and customer loyalty.

## 6. Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
- **Definition**: Average total revenue contributed by a customer across all observed transactions.
- **Formula**: `SUM(amount by customer_id) averaged across customers`
- **Data Source**: Transactions table (`customer_id`, `amount`)
- **Target Range**: $250 - $500
- **Owner**: Finance Lead
- **Update Frequency**: Monthly
- **Notes**: Used for prioritizing acquisition spend and retention investment.

## 7. Payment Success Rate
- **Definition**: Share of payment attempts that complete successfully.
- **Formula**: `Successful payments / Total payment attempts`
- **Data Source**: Payments table (`payment_status` or `is_payment_successful`)
- **Target Range**: 95% - 100%
- **Owner**: Engineering Lead
- **Update Frequency**: Daily
- **Notes**: Core checkout reliability metric; should be monitored with alerting.

## 8. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- **Definition**: Average cost to acquire one customer.
- **Formula**: `Total acquisition spend / New customers acquired`
- **Data Source**: Marketing spend table (`acquisition_cost`) and customer table (`customer_id`)
- **Target Range**: $0 - $50
- **Owner**: Marketing Lead
- **Update Frequency**: Weekly
- **Notes**: Should stay below customer lifetime value to preserve unit economics.

## Governance Rules
- Use the same KPI functions for all dashboards and reports.
- When a KPI definition changes, restate historical values and document the revision date.
- Targets should be reviewed quarterly against historical performance and business goals.
- Any KPI that lacks a source table must be marked as a dependency and not estimated silently.
