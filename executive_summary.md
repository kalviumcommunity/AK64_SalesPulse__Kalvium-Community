# CHURN REDUCTION INITIATIVE
## Executive Summary

### Situation
Customer churn is SalesPulse’s leading revenue loss driver, costing **$2.0M annually**. Our current annual churn rate sits at **7.0%** against an industry benchmark of 4.0%. Root cause analysis reveals support response delay as the primary driver of customer cancellations, creating a high-impact revenue recovery opportunity for Q1.

### Key Findings
- **Response Speed Drives Retention**: Customers receiving support within 2 hours churn at **3.0%**. Customers waiting over 24 hours churn at **12.0%** (a **4x increase** in cancellation risk).
- **Primary Operational Bottleneck**: Our support team currently averages a **6.2-hour response time**, causing customer dissatisfaction during critical workflows.
- **High-Value Account Vulnerability**: Premium accounts spending >$10K/year churn at **15.0%** when support is slow, exposing high-margin recurring revenue.

### Business Risks
- **Revenue at Risk**: Doing nothing costs **$2.0M annually** in preventable churn. High-value customer relationships are especially vulnerable.
- **Competitive Vulnerability**: Better-supported competitors actively target our dissatisfied accounts; acquiring a replacement customer costs **5x more** than retaining an existing one.
- **Operational Degradation**: Support ticket volume is up **40% YoY** while staffing has remained flat, creating severe burnout and queue delays.

### Recommendations
1. **Hire 2 Support Engineers** (*Cost: $200K/year*)
   - Expands peak coverage and reduces average response time from 6.2 hours to **<2 hours**.
   - **Impact**: Recovers **$400K in annual churn reduction** (**2.0x ROI** in Year 1).

2. **Implement 2-Hour Response SLA** (*Cost: $0*)
   - Establish formal <2 hour first-response SLA with real-time dashboard tracking to enforce team accountability.
   - **Timeline**: Effective Jan 1.

3. **Prioritize High-Value Accounts** (*Cost: $50K engineering*)
   - Deploy automated CRM routing to direct >$10K/year accounts to a dedicated priority queue.
   - **Impact**: Reduces high-value segment churn by **50%**, protecting **$250K in revenue**.
   - **Timeline**: Effective Feb 1.
